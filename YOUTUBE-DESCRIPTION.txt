--- LONG DESCRIPTION (main video) ---

Every other visual on your page tells you how much. A histogram tells you how it is
spread — where the mass sits, how far the tail runs, and whether there are two
populations hiding inside what looks like one.

Power BI has no native histogram. The usual workaround is a calculated column of bins in
DAX: it fixes the bin count at design time, has to be rewritten to change it, and gives
you no mean, no median and no quartiles. Histogram Pro is two field wells and no
formulas.

WHAT YOU SEE IN THIS VIDEO
Setting it up on a real dataset, choosing a bin count, reading a skewed distribution, and
the reference lines that make it interpretable — mean, median, quartiles, a benchmark and
the normal curve.

WHAT IT DOES
• The distribution over your whole table, not a sample — verified at 500,000 rows
• Mean and median lines, quartiles and IQR shading, without a line of DAX
• An axis built for skewed data: it focuses on where the data lives instead of following
  a single extreme value, and every row is still counted
• A benchmark line for a target or SLA
• IBCS monochrome mode for standards-based reporting
• One click filters the report by every row behind the bar
• Drill-down, report page tooltips, keyboard navigation and high contrast

FREE vs PRO
The free tier is the histogram itself, over your whole dataset, with no watermark and no
row limit of ours.

Free:
• The distribution, over the whole table
• Mean and median lines, quartiles and IQR shading
• Benchmark line, legend and IBCS mode
• Cross-filtering, drill-down, keyboard and high contrast

Pro:
• Bin count anywhere from 2 to 100, instead of a fixed 10
• Outlier trimming, when you want to set the range yourself
• The statistics panel: n, mean, median, standard deviation, min and max
• Normal curve overlay
• Value labels on the bars
• Bar colour, opacity, border and gap

Every paid setting says (Pro) in the format pane and is never hidden. Turn one on without
a licence and Power BI shows its own notification with the link to obtain one — the
setting is kept and applies as soon as the licence is active.

30-day free trial on AppSource. No card required.

PRIVACY
No network calls of any kind: no telemetry, no analytics, no CDN, no endpoints. All
calculation happens inside Power BI.
