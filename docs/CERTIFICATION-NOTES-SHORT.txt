Histogram Pro 1.3.0.0 - GUID HistogramPro38A7C1D94E2B4F6D8A0E5B3C9F1D2E4A
WHAT CHANGED IN 1.3.0.0
1. PRO PREVIEW. A free user who turned on a Pro setting saw NOTHING happen: it was silently
   reverted. That reads as a broken visual, not as something to buy. Now, editing without a
   licence and once it has resolved, the feature is drawn WORKING under a "Pro preview"
   watermark naming it, granted per feature, never in bulk. In reading view, before the
   licence resolves, and where it cannot be read (Publish to Web, embed, export): free
   result, no watermark.
2. The Upgrade bar no longer wipes out the feature banner: Power BI shows one notification
   at a time and both were raised back to back. The banner comes first and the Upgrade bar
   10.5 s later. Cancelled in destroy().
3. Fixed: the notice never cleared. Whether a feature was "attempted" was read from the
   presence of the property in metadata.objects, which Power BI keeps forever once written,
   even after returning it to its free value. It now compares the VALUE.
4. Fixed: no repaint once the licence resolved to Free, so the preview never appeared and
   vanished on returning to the page.
5. BUILD: four fetch calls from d3-fetch were bundled. tsconfig had module=commonjs, which
   defeats tree-shaking, so all of d3 came in although the visual calls none of it. Now
   module=esnext and --certification-audit reports none. Toolchain current (tools 7.2.1,
   API 5.11.1), npm audit 0, lint clean - it was being skipped.

PRIVACY: no network calls, no telemetry, no storage. Only the dataView. privileges [].

FREE VS PRO
Free: the histogram with 10 bins, mean, median, percentile and IQR markers, benchmark line,
axes, legend, IBCS mode, tooltips, cross-filtering, high contrast.
Pro: bin count, outlier trimming, bar styling, statistics panel, normal curve, value labels.
