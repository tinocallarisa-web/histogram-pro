Histogram Pro 1.2.0.0. Full notes: CERTIFICATION-NOTES.md in the repo.

Correctness. The reduction was "top": with a measure bound it selects the highest N, so
past 30,000 rows only the upper tail was drawn. Now "window" plus segment streaming via
fetchMoreData - the full distribution, verified at 500,000 rows. The x-axis ran min..max,
so one extreme value buried every row in the first bin; it now uses Tukey's fences, only
when they narrow the range. No rows are dropped: values beyond the axis are counted in the
end bar and the chart says so.

Licensing. checkLicense() resolved async and never repainted, so a paying customer could
keep the free tier in a report that renders once. Fixed. Warning accepted alongside Active
(grace period). isLicenseUnsupportedEnv and isLicenseInfoAvailable honoured: Publish to
Web, embedding and PDF export no longer read a Pro customer as Free.

Purchase path. The Free caption inside the chart is gone: own licensing UI, a watermark
with nothing to click. Replaced by notifyFeatureBlocked and notifyLicenseRequired, which
carry the link. Intent read from metadata.objects: only what the user set.

Cross-filtering. A BasicFilter over the rows in the bin: up to 10,000 distinct values in
one click, the practical ceiling over a live connection. Above that it declines and names
the lever, rather than filtering a subset and giving a silently wrong answer.

allowInteractions is checked before selecting, clearing and the menu.

LICENCE
Official IVisualLicenseManager only, async, never blocks render. No licence server, no external
auth, no payment in the visual. No watermark, no artificial free limits.

DATA
Only the categorical dataView (Values, Detail rows, Tooltips). No fetch, no XHR, no
local file access.

TIERS
Free: the histogram with 10 bins, mean and median, quartiles and IQR, benchmark,
legend, IBCS mode, axes, cross-filtering, keyboard, high contrast.
Pro: bins 2-100, outlier trimming, statistics panel, normal curve, value labels, bar
styling.

TEST
Bind a numeric measure to Values and the row-level field to Detail (rows). Without a
licence, change the bin count or turn on the statistics panel: the free result stays and
Power BI raises its notification with the purchase link. Remove it and it clears.
Right-click a bar and empty space: context menu on both.
