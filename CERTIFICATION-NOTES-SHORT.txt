Histogram Pro 1.2.1.0. Full notes: CERTIFICATION-NOTES.md in the repo.

WHY THIS RESUBMISSION
Replaces 1.2.0.0. Licence fix: spIdentifier is the full Service ID (publisher.offer.plan);
the check now accepts one ending in ".histogram-pro-tcviz". A failed licence lookup no
longer shows purchase prompts.

SINCE 1.1.0.0 (unchanged from 1.2.0.0)
- Correctness: "window" reduction plus fetchMoreData streaming draws the full
  distribution (verified at 500,000 rows). The x-axis uses Tukey's fences only when they
  narrow the range; values beyond the axis are counted in the end bar and labelled.
- Licensing: resolved async and repaints when it arrives. Active and Warning accepted.
  isLicenseUnsupportedEnv and isLicenseInfoAvailable honoured.
- Purchase path: no in-chart caption; notifyFeatureBlocked and notifyLicenseRequired,
  fired from metadata.objects (only what the user set).
- Cross-filtering: BasicFilter over the rows in the bin, up to 10,000 distinct values;
  above that it declines and says why instead of filtering a subset.
- allowInteractions checked before selecting, clearing and the context menu.

LICENCE
Official IVisualLicenseManager only, async, never blocks render. No licence server,
external auth or payment in the visual. No watermark.

DATA
Categorical dataView only (Values, Detail rows, Tooltips). No fetch, XHR or local file
access.

TIERS
Free: 10 bins, mean and median, quartiles and IQR, benchmark, legend, IBCS mode, axes,
cross-filtering, keyboard, high contrast.
Pro: bins 2-100, outlier trimming, statistics panel, normal curve, value labels, bar
styling.

TEST
Bind a numeric measure to Values and the row-level field to Detail (rows). Without a
licence, change the bin count or turn on the statistics panel: the free result stays and
Power BI shows its notification with the purchase link; remove it and it clears.
Right-click a bar and empty space: context menu on both.
